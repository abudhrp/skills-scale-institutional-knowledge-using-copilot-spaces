# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives serve as the voice of end-users and business stakeholders. They bridge the gap between the business and delivery teams by participating in requirements gathering, solution validation, and user acceptance testing.

### Responsibilities
- Represent end-user and business stakeholder interests throughout the project lifecycle
- Participate in requirements gathering sessions and provide business context
- Review and validate solutions against business needs and acceptance criteria
- Provide feedback during solution demonstrations and testing phases
- Communicate project status and outcomes back to their stakeholder group
- Escalate business concerns or misalignments to Project Manager and Product Manager

### Key Interactions
- **With Product Manager**: Collaborates on defining user needs and validating product direction
- **With Project Manager**: Participates in planning and provides business input on scope and priorities
- **With Developers**: Reviews technical solutions for business alignment and usability
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Participates in user acceptance testing and validates quality standards

### Goals
- Ensure solutions meet stakeholder and end-user expectations
- Reduce rework due to misaligned requirements
- Enable smooth adoption and user satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Requirements sessions and feedback workshops
- Solution demonstration reviews
- User acceptance testing sessions
- Status updates to their stakeholder group

---

## Subject Matter Expert (SME)

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide specialized knowledge in specific domains. They advise on technical and business feasibility, help resolve complex questions, and ensure solutions align with domain best practices and standards.

### Responsibilities
- Provide expert guidance on domain-specific requirements and constraints
- Advise on technical and business feasibility of proposed solutions
- Review design and implementation decisions for domain compliance
- Identify domain-specific risks and recommend mitigations
- Help resolve complex technical or business questions that arise during delivery
- Ensure solutions align with industry standards and organizational policies

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Provides technical guidance and reviews implementation decisions
- **With Technical Architect**: Collaborates on solution design to ensure alignment with domain standards
- **With Project Manager**: Advises on feasibility and domain-related risk management
- **With Product Manager**: Provides domain expertise to inform product decisions

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are technically sound and domain-compliant
- Reduce costly rework due to misalignment with domain standards
- Share domain knowledge across the team

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical consultations
- Risk assessment and mitigation discussions
- Documentation of domain-specific guidance and constraints
- One-on-one consultations with team members

---

## Compliance & Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance & Risk Officers ensure projects adhere to organizational policies, regulatory requirements, and risk management standards. They identify compliance risks, guide mitigation strategies, and ensure proper governance is maintained throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Identify compliance and regulatory requirements relevant to the project
- Conduct risk assessments and maintain risk registers with the Project Manager
- Review project plans and solutions for compliance with organizational policies
- Guide risk mitigation strategies and escalate critical risks
- Ensure proper governance and audit trails are maintained
- Coordinate with legal and regulatory teams as needed
- Provide compliance checkpoints during project planning and execution

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Partners on risk management and governance coordination
- **With Developers**: Reviews technical solutions for security and compliance requirements
- **With Technical Architect**: Advises on architecture and design for compliance and security
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Ensures compliance is validated in testing

### Goals
- Prevent compliance violations and regulatory issues
- Reduce project-level risks through early identification and mitigation
- Maintain organizational standards and governance

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessment workshops and reviews
- Compliance checklists and audit documentation
- Risk register maintenance and escalation reports
- Governance and decision documentation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads oversee testing strategy, quality standards, and defect management throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and quality standards for the project
- Plan and execute testing activities (functional, performance, security, compliance)
- Identify, track, and prioritize defects and quality issues
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria and test case development
- Validate that deliverables meet quality gates before release
- Report on quality metrics and testing progress
- Recommend process improvements based on quality findings

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Collaborates on test planning and participates in design reviews
- **With Product Manager**: Ensures acceptance criteria are clear and testable
- **With Project Manager**: Provides quality status updates and recommends schedule adjustments based on quality risks
- **With Stakeholder Representative**: Conducts user acceptance testing and gathers feedback

### Goals
- Ensure deliverables meet quality and acceptance criteria
- Reduce defects and issues released to production
- Build confidence in solution quality across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Test plan and strategy documents
- Defect reports and quality dashboards
- Testing status updates in project reviews
- Design and acceptance criteria reviews

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design technical solutions and ensure alignment with enterprise architecture standards, scalability requirements, and organizational technology strategies. They guide technical decisions and help navigate complex technical trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions that meet functional and non-functional requirements
- Ensure solutions align with enterprise architecture standards and guidelines
- Review and guide technical implementation decisions
- Identify technical risks and recommend mitigations
- Plan for scalability, performance, security, and maintainability
- Document architectural decisions and trade-offs
- Support technology selection and integration planning

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Provides architectural guidance and reviews implementation decisions
- **With Technical Architect**: Ensures consistency with enterprise standards and future roadmap
- **With Product Manager**: Advises on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **With Project Manager**: Identifies technical dependencies and risks
- **With Subject Matter Expert**: Collaborates on domain-specific architectural requirements

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Ensure consistency with organizational technology strategy
- Reduce technical debt and future refactoring costs

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design documents and diagrams
- Technical design reviews and decision documentation
- Risk and dependency identification
- Technology evaluation and selection guidance

---

## Change Management Lead

### Role Summary
Change Management Leads plan and execute user adoption, communication, and change readiness activities. They ensure stakeholders understand, accept, and effectively adopt the solution being delivered.

### Responsibilities
- Develop comprehensive change management and adoption plans
- Create and execute stakeholder communication strategies
- Assess organizational readiness for change and identify resistance
- Design and deliver training and enablement programs
- Support user adoption and provide change support during and after go-live
- Measure change adoption and effectiveness
- Document lessons learned and identify areas for improvement

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Manager**: Coordinates change activities as part of overall project plan
- **With Stakeholder Representative**: Works with stakeholders to understand adoption concerns
- **With Product Manager**: Ensures communication aligns with product vision and value proposition
- **With Developers**: Coordinates technical training and documentation
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Supports user acceptance testing and user readiness

### Goals
- Maximize user adoption and solution effectiveness
- Minimize disruption and resistance during implementation
- Ensure long-term success of the delivered solution

### Typical Communication
- Change management plans and communication strategies
- Training materials and enablement programs
- Adoption dashboards and feedback mechanisms
- Stakeholder engagement sessions and town halls

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Supporting personas (Stakeholder Representative, SME, Compliance & Risk Officer, QA Lead, Technical Architect, and Change Management Lead) add richness to project scenarios by representing the broader ecosystem of roles involved in successful project delivery.
